# Offline Study watch page references

- **Crunchyroll** documents a Skip Intro action that appears in the player when the opening begins. Playback speed and autoplay live under the player’s settings gear. Its public release calendar identifies episodes by number and title and includes viewing progress. [Skip Intro](https://help.crunchyroll.com/hc/en-us/articles/20369940738708-What-is-the-Skip-Intro-feature) · [Playback speed](https://help.crunchyroll.com/hc/en-us/articles/38575543440020-How-do-I-change-my-playback-speed) · [Website guide](https://help.crunchyroll.com/hc/en-us/articles/22728708616852-Getting-started-on-Crunchyroll-website) · [Release calendar](https://www.crunchyroll.com/simulcastcalendar?filter=premium)
- **Plex** places previous and next actions alongside playback controls in its web player. Starting a TV episode can also populate a queue with the remaining episodes. [Web player](https://support.plex.tv/articles/200392226-plex-web-app-player/) · [Play queues](https://support.plex.tv/articles/202188298-play-queues/)
- **Netflix** offers configurable autoplay for the next episode. **YouTube** uses labeled chapters and timestamps to make parts of a video easy to revisit. [Netflix autoplay](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/121518) · [YouTube chapters](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)
- **Casting** uses Chrome’s tab casting to share Offline Study, including the subtitle and player overlays. [Offline casting research](offline-casting.md) · [Cast from Chrome](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/3228332?hl=en)

For Offline Study, make the video the dominant element and keep the current episode and next episode close to it. A local episode list gives viewers a way to jump directly to another download. Use the detected opening, ending, and recap ranges as labeled jump points. Keep file selection and download setup in the library area so they remain available while viewing. These are design decisions for the offline workflow drawn from the patterns above.
