JAPANESE LISTENING

Learn while you watch.

Open a Japanese video on YouTube or smotret-anime in Safari.

  1. Allow this extension on the video page and its player.
  2. Tap 日本語 Learn on the page.
  3. Enable Pause for listening quizzes.

Japanese subtitles are found automatically when the player makes them available. Your answers schedule future reviews.

Selected phrases may be sent to Google Translate for answer choices. Your review history stays on this device.
